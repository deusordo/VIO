Public update tracking

The prototype is designed to read public publisher feeds on a schedule and preserve links for follow-up. A prior test checked a feed item against its publisher, read the stored entry back, and confirmed that a repeat run skipped the duplicate.

A publisher check supports a record of what was published. A broader factual claim still needs evidence and review. Feed collection requires a connection to the publisher.

Questions about VIO

What can I try today?
Download the sample and open it in a browser after disconnecting. VIO’s broader tools are still at the prototype stage.
Do I need a server or an AI model?
The sample needs only a browser. A shared collection needs a local host and storage. Local AI is optional and its hardware needs depend on the model.
What happens when the internet drops?
Saved references remain readable. Fresh feeds and remote sources become available again when the connection returns.
Where do the books and guides fit?
NuMind curates reading resources and plans the library for VATRA. VIO works on ways to package, serve, and recover reference material.
