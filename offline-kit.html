Offline readiness checklist

Save or print this page. Keep household contact details in a protected copy with the people who need them.

Build and practice during ordinary weeks

  • Pick one essential service. Write down its owner, backup person, and what must still work when the internet or power is out.
  • Save one map, one repair or operating manual, and a printed index. Put private meeting points and contacts in the protected household plan.
  • Ask another person to locate the instructions with the internet off. Record what they needed, fix the missing step, and repeat.

Use the fallback during an emergency

  • Check official alerts and follow the guidance on shelter or evacuation. Have an alternate way to receive updates if phones or internet fail.
  • Use the household check-in plan. Keep messages short and include when and where to check in next.
  • Record which essentials are working and which people need follow-up. Share personal information only with the people responsible for helping.

Keep essentials going through a long disruption

  • Track safe water, sanitation, food, warmth, and access to care. Agree on replenishment, a backup, and a second person who can run each service.
  • Work with trusted neighbors and local responders to coordinate supplies and check-ins. Save paper copies where they can be found without power.
  • After service returns, record what failed, what worked, and the time spent. Fix the weakest step and run a fresh practice test.
