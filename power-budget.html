Total daily demand
  1. Daily demand = sum of watts × hours. A directly measured charge in Wh can be added to the daily total.
  2. Days on battery alone = usable output Wh ÷ daily demand in Wh. With the sample measurements, 96 ÷ 44 = 2.18 days.
  3. Solar Wh/day = rated watts × equivalent full-sun hours × charging yield. The example is 30 × 2 × 0.75 = 45 Wh/day.
  4. Log the measured yield on a poor-weather day. If it is below demand, reduce the load or test another fallback before relying on it.

Before relying on a system

Use compatible, manufacturer-approved portable equipment. Permanent solar, building circuits, and backup for critical equipment need an installation and continuity plan appropriate to the site. Get qualified help before changing building wiring.
